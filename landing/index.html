The New Global Standard

The world’s largest media platforms are adopting this payment rail

In April 2026, Meta launched USDC creator payouts built on Circle’s infrastructure. Instant, borderless settlement in digital dollars is officially the new industry benchmark.

What they proved

Digital dollars are how creators get paid now

Meta rolled it out through Stripe on Circle's USDC, expanding across more than 160 markets. Nobody has to be convinced that paying creators in stablecoins works — platforms with billions of users have already validated it.

What is still missing

Nothing there waits to see if the views were real

Faster payment on unverified numbers is just faster payment for bought views. We count, wait a day, count again, and pay on the lower figure. That is the critical layer nobody else provides, ensuring brands only pay for genuine engagement.

We are not proposing an unproven payment mechanism. We build on the exact same Circle USDC rails used by global tech leaders, fortified with a 24-hour verification hold.

Standing

Your score is just your own numbers

The same figure we pay you on, shown back to you.

Other platforms give you a trust score built on ratings and approval rates. Those measure how the platform feels about you, and you can push them up by posting a lot of anything.

Ours is just maths. Your standing is how many of your views were still there when the wait ended — the exact number we pay you on. You cannot game it by posting more, because buying views pushes it down: the fake ones disappear, and they still count against you.

Unproven

Fewer than three clips counted

You have not proved anything yet — and nor has anyone else this early. We would rather say that than hand you a score based on one video.

Reliable

Most of your views stick

Brands look for this. It is the difference between a real audience and numbers that show up fast and vanish overnight.

Exceptional

Almost nothing you post disappears

If you have never bought a view, you land here without trying. If you buy them, you cannot — however much you post.

FAQ

Your questions, answered

The things creators ask before their first clip.

Why wait?Why 24 hours, when other platforms pay sooner?

Because platforms delete fake views after the fact, usually within a day. Paying before that happens is how brands end up buying bot views without knowing. The wait is the whole point — if you want paying the second you post, we are the wrong platform on purpose.

Falling viewsWhat if my view count drops?

We pay on the lowest count we saw during the wait, so a drop lowers what you are owed. We never take money back: what you have been paid is yours, and a falling count just means a smaller next payment, never a negative one.

Getting paidWhen do I get paid, and is there a minimum?

Usually minutes after the wait ends, straight to your wallet. No minimum, nothing to withdraw, and no balance sitting with us — your own wallet is where it lands.

FeesIs anything taken from my earnings?

No. We charge the brand a flat fee per campaign and per payment, never a slice of your earnings. A percentage would mean we earn more when a brand pays for bots.

RejectionsWhy was my clip not paid?

Every refusal tells you why, in words. A clip still inside its waiting period says so and is not a rejection. A clip that missed the brief says which part it missed.

USDCWhat is USDC?

1 USDC = $1, always. It is a digital dollar, issued by a regulated company called Circle that holds the actual dollars backing it. It is not a cryptocurrency that swings in value — the whole point is that it does not. It is a dollar that can move over the internet in seconds.

You can hold it, send it, or convert it to your local currency through most exchanges.

Why USDCWhy pay in that rather than into my bank?

Because it arrives, wherever you are. A bank transfer from a brand in one country to a creator in another takes days, costs a slice, and sometimes quietly fails. This lands in minutes and costs cents to send.

It also means we never hold your money. Payment goes straight from the brand's wallet to yours. There is no balance sitting with us, no minimum to reach before you can withdraw, and nothing for us to freeze — which is the part most creators have been burned by somewhere else.

AccountsDo I need an account, and why Google?

You sign in with Google so your standing follows you instead of resetting every time you use a different wallet. We store no password and ask Google for nothing beyond your name and email.

Worth being straight about the limit: an account makes it harder to dodge the per-creator cap by using many wallets, but it does not make it impossible, because Google accounts are easy to get. Anyone who tells you their sign-in stops fraud outright is selling something.

PlatformsWhich platforms can I post on?

YouTube today. Instagram, Facebook and TikTok need approvals we do not have yet, and we would rather turn a link away than promise a check we cannot actually do.

Agencies

Agencies

One campaign per client, and nothing pools across them.

If you run campaigns for several brands, each is a separate campaign with its own budget, its own wallet and its own frozen terms. Nothing pools across clients, so one brand's spend can never reach another's creators, and each budget is verifiable on-chain independently.

The same API the app uses is public and documented, so a campaign can be opened, funded and monitored programmatically rather than through this page. Read the API contract →
