Get paid in three steps

From submission to payment, with the one delay that makes it work.

1

Post your clip

Pick a live campaign and submit your link. Your CPM, hold and cap lock the moment it is accepted — the brand cannot move them afterwards.

2

The hold

We read the count from the platform, never from you, and read it again a day later. Bought views are gone by then. Yours are not.

3

Get paid

USDC to your own wallet, for every view that survived. No balance sits with us, so there is no minimum and nothing to cash out.

Standing you earn, not a score we award

Every platform here ships a trust score built on ratings and approval rates. Those measure how a platform feels about you, and they can be farmed by posting a lot of anything.

Yours is arithmetic. Your standing is the share of your views that were still there when the hold closed — the same number that decides your payout, shown back to you. It cannot be farmed by volume, because buying views lowers it: the bought ones evaporate and land in the denominator.

Exceptional

Protection that is checked, not promised

Each of these is enforced in code and verified by deliberately breaking it to confirm a test fails. None of them depends on us behaving well.

For brands

A budget that cannot be exceeded

Total payouts never exceed the campaign budget, and no creator can take more than their cap. The agent may allocate inside a rate band; it can never widen it.

For brands

You are charged only for what survived

Views that vanish before the hold closes are never paid for. That is the mechanic, not a detection system we ask you to trust.

For creators

Terms freeze when your clip is accepted

Rate, hold and cap are copied onto your submission. A brand pausing or ending a campaign refuses new clips — it does not abandon work already taken.

For creators

Budgets you can check yourself

Every campaign names the wallet its money sits in, and we read the on-chain balance. A campaign that has not funded is labelled unfunded before you spend an evening on it.

Your questions, answered

Why wait?

Why 24 hours, when other platforms pay sooner?

Because platforms remove inauthentic views retroactively, usually within a day. Paying before that happens is why brands buy bot views without knowing. The wait is the product — if you want paying the second you post, we are the wrong platform, deliberately.

Falling views

What if my view count drops?

You are paid on the lowest count observed across the window, so a drop reduces what is owed. It is never clawed back: money already settled stays yours, and a falling count reduces the next payment rather than producing a negative one.

Getting paid

When do I get paid, and is there a minimum?

Usually minutes after the hold closes, straight to your wallet. There is no minimum and nothing to withdraw, because no balance ever sits with us.

Fees

Is anything taken from my earnings?

No. We charge brands per campaign and per settled payout, never a percentage of spend — a percentage would mean our revenue rises when a brand pays for bots.

Rejections

Why was my clip not paid?

Every refusal names its reason in words. A clip still inside its hold says so and is not a rejection; a clip that missed the brief says which part it missed.

Platforms

Which platforms can I post on?

YouTube today. Instagram, Facebook and TikTok need platform review we do not hold, and we would rather refuse a link than promise a check we cannot perform.

What this is not

  • Not a bot detector. We do not claim to out-detect fraud farms. We changed what gets bought so that outlasting them is unnecessary.
  • Not a bank. No balance is held for you, which is why there is no cash-out and no minimum.
  • Not instant. The hold is the point.
  • Early. Settlement is proven on testnet. You would be among the first campaigns.
