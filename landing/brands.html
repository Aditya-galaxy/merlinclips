The problem

You cannot tell which half of a campaign was real

So we changed what is being bought.

Nobody can, and that is the problem. Everyone in this market pays on a number the person getting paid can buy for less than the payment. Trying to spot the fakes loses that race, because the money goes out before anyone can tell.

So we changed what you are buying. We count the views, count them again a day later, and charge you the lower number. Faking a view is cheap. Keeping it alive for a whole day, while the platform is busy deleting fake ones, is not.

Verification

Why verification matters here

A model watches the clip and decides, before any money is authorised.

On other platforms, "verified" means a badge on a profile. Here it means a model watched the clip and decided whether it does what you asked for — before any money is approved.

The brief

Judged against your words, not a checklist

You write the brief in plain language — show the product in the first five seconds — and each clip is checked against it. No rule expresses that; a model can.

Injection

A clip that argues with the verifier still fails

We built an attack clip carrying on-screen text instructing the verifier to pass it unexamined. It was refused, and the refusal named the timestamps. More importantly the verdict is one precondition among several: a suborned verdict still cannot exceed your budget, your per-creator cap, or the hold.

Counts

Numbers come from the platform, never the creator

View counts are read from the platform's own API. A creator telling us what they earned is not evidence, and we never ask.

FAQ

Answers to your questions

What brands ask before funding a first campaign.

PricingWhat do you charge?

Per campaign and per settled payout — never a percentage of spend. A percentage would mean our revenue rises when you pay for bots, and we would rather not hold that incentive at all than promise to resist it.

FundingDo I fund before or after?

Before. The remaining budget is published to creators as the amount left to earn, and a budget with no money behind it is a promise nobody checked. Unfunded campaigns are labelled as such on the listing.

SpeedWhy does settlement take a day?

Because that is roughly how long platforms take to remove inauthentic views. Paying sooner means paying before anyone can tell. The delay is the product.

ControlCan I stop a campaign?

Yes — and it stops accepting new clips immediately. It does not abandon work already accepted: a creator who posted under your terms is still owed for views that survive, until the settlement deadline. That guarantee is why creators take the brief seriously.

PlatformsWhich platforms are supported?

YouTube today. Instagram, Facebook and TikTok need platform review we do not hold, and we would rather refuse a link than promise a check we cannot perform.
